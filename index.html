    Eoin's Building Game

    A first-person voxel sandbox. Build freely in Creative with twenty-one kinds of block and blueprints, or play Survival: gather, craft tools, keep yourself fed and hold out against the creatures that roam at night.

    How to play
    MoveW A S D · Space to jump · Shift to sprint · F to toggle flying
    LookMove the mouse (click the world to capture the pointer)
    BuildLeft click places the selected block · Right click breaks · Middle click picks the block you are looking at
    BlocksMouse wheel or keys 1–9 select · Tab shows the second page of blocks
    BlueprintsB opens blueprint mode · scroll or [ ] to choose · R rotates · left click stamps the structure
    SurvivalHold right click to mine · C opens crafting · left click eats food or attacks with the held tool · watch your health and hunger
    TimeA full day lasts four minutes · N skips ahead to dusk or dawn
    MenuEsc pauses · the world saves automatically and can be exported as JSON

    Everything here is drawn and synthesised in code: no image or audio files are used.
